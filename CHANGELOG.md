## Unreleased

- The menus play a live CPU race behind them, filmed with TV-style camera cuts.
- Racer select: every player picks with up and down on their own cursor, A locks in, B unlocks; phones tap a racer on their screen.
- The lobby shows the phone join QR code too.
- Karts slide along walls instead of stopping dead.
- Phone pad: controls stay clear of the iPhone notch and home bar; a stale QR says so after 10 s.
- Every player shows a name: your phone name, your Steam name, or the pad's name.
- Karts now handle like real (arcade) cars: they roll where the wheels point, slide on tyre grip, and drift by letting the rear go.
- A minimap in every corner of the race, north up, with crates, hazards and the start line.
- New menus: no text runs out of its box, and the title shows the version and What's new.
- Phone controllers work from any network: scan the QR, no shared Wi-Fi needed.
- Drifting is subtler: wider arcs, no donuts, only above 40% speed.
- Each phone is its own player with its own seat, colour and HUD badge.
- 2-4 player split screen with a join lobby.
- Calmer camera and gentler steering, with camera options.
- Music: your own files or the built-in CC0 bluegrass soundtrack.
- Wailing Whiskers joins the roster.
